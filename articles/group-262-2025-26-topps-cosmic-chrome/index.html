❓ What's the difference between hobby boxes and retail boxes for basketball cards?
Hobby boxes guarantee minimum autograph hits (typically 2–4 per box) and feature improved card centering, justifying their $120–$180 price versus retail packs at $4–$6. Hobby boxes source from the same manufacturers but follow stricter quality control and hit-rate verification. Sealed hobby boxes appreciate faster than retail packs.
❓ How do I know if basketball trading cards are authentic?
Authentic cards from Topps and Bowman display crisp print quality, even centering, and consistent cardstock weight. Counterfeits show blurry printing, uneven borders, and flimsy materials. Purchase sealed boxes only from authorized retailers (target: Amazon, eBay verified sellers, or manufacturer-authorized distributors). Third-party authentication services like PSA confirm authenticity for high-value cards.
