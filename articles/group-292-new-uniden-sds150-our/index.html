❓ What is the difference between digital and analog radio scanners?
Digital scanners decode digital transmission modes (P25, DMR, NXDN) using advanced signal processing, while analog scanners receive only traditional FM broadcasts. Digital scanners offer better selectivity, reject interference more effectively, and provide crystal-clear audio in crowded frequency environments, making them superior for modern emergency services monitoring.
❓ Do I need a license to operate a radio scanner?
In the United States and Canada, receiving radio transmissions is generally legal without licensing for personal use.
