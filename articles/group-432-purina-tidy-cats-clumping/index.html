Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Optimal litter box management requires consistent maintenance regardless of formulation: scoop clumping litter daily to prevent ammonia buildup and bacterial growth; refresh non-clumping crystal boxes every 10–14 days or when saturation exceeds 50% of the box. Store all litter in cool, dry conditions to prevent moisture activation and clay caking. For multi-cat households, the general rule is one box per cat plus one extra—each filled with 3–4 inches of litter—to reduce territorial marking and ensure consistent waste containment. Monitor your cat's bathroom frequency; sudden changes in urine output or frequency warrant veterinary consultation, especially in senior cats. Gradually transition between litter types over 5–7 days by blending old and new products to prevent litter box avoidance due to unfamiliar texture or scent.
