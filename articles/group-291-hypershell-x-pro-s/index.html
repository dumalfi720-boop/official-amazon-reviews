Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maintain consistent calibration by running the 'Gait Sync' diagnostic every 200 operating hours—this 8-minute process ensures the 0.31-second response time stays optimally tuned to your individual walking pattern. Store batteries at 40–60% charge in cool, dry conditions when not using your exoskeleton for extended periods; this extends cell lifespan from 1200 to 1600+ charge cycles. Clean the soft package weekly with mild soap and lukewarm water to prevent sweat-induced degradation of elastic materials. Inspect the motor bearings monthly for debris accumulation, particularly after outdoor terrain use. Rotate between two exoskeletons if possible to extend lifespan; continuous daily operation accelerates wear on joint stabilizers. Charge batteries after use drops below 25%—avoiding deep discharge preserves overall capacity more effectively than opportunistic charging.
