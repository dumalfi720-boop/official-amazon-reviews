Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Establish consistent power management practices: use manufacturer-supplied power adapters exclusively to prevent voltage delivery inconsistencies, implement weekly shutdown cycles for firmware stability, and maintain 15-20% battery charge minimum during storage periods. Enable Legion Control Center power profiles matching your workload—"Quiet" mode for office work reduces fan noise, while "Performance" mode unlocks thermal capabilities for competitive gaming. Schedule regular firmware updates during powered operation to prevent interrupted BIOS rewriting that can create startup failures. Maintain adequate ambient ventilation and clean air intake vents monthly to prevent thermal throttling.
