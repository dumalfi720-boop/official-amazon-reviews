What Is Eletronicos?

Mirrorless cameras refer to interchangeable-lens digital cameras that use an electronic viewfinder instead of a traditional mirror mechanism, offering compact designs with advanced autofocus systems, real-time exposure preview, and high-speed continuous shooting capabilities. These systems have become industry standard for professional and enthusiast photographers seeking hybrid flexibility between speed and portability.
