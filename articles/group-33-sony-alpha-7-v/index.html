What Is Eletronicos?

Mirrorless cameras refer to interchangeable-lens digital cameras that eliminate the traditional reflex mirror mechanism, using electronic viewfinders and sensor-based autofocus instead. This architecture enables compact designs, real-time exposure preview, and advanced computational photography features, making them increasingly popular among professionals and enthusiasts seeking versatile imaging solutions.
