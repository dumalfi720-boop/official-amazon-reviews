❓ Do portable tow brakes work with all vehicles?
Portable tow brakes work with most vehicles manufactured after 1995 with accessible brake lines and electrical systems. Pre-1995 vehicles, some European imports, and specialized truck configurations may require custom adapters costing $50-200. Always verify compatibility using the manufacturer's vehicle database before purchasing, as incompatible installations damage brake systems and void warranties.
❓ How often should you maintain a portable tow brake system?
Inspect brake fluid levels monthly and replace fluid every 24 months or per manufacturer specifications. Perform comprehensive pressure tests annually before each towing season, confirming response times match baseline measurements. Store systems in climate-controlled conditions and protect electrical connectors with silicone dielectric grease. Most well-maintained systems require minimal component replacement within 10 years.
❓ What is the difference between vacuum and hydraulic tow brakes?
Vacuum brakes use engine vacuum to activate braking, offering simpler installation but requiring sufficient engine vacuum pressure—problematic for diesel engines. Hydraulic brakes use brake fluid pressure for activation, delivering faster response times (0.3-0.5 seconds) and superior consistency across temperature extremes. Modern portable systems favor hydraulic design, though some older RVs still use vacuum configurations effectively for light towing applications.
