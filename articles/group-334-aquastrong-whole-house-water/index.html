Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maintain your water pressure booster pump by checking pressure gauge readings monthly (target 40-80 PSI for residential systems). Drain the pressurized tank annually to prevent mineral sediment accumulation, particularly in hard-water regions. Inspect rubber isolation mounts quarterly, replacing if cracked, to maintain quiet operation. For seasonal closures, shut off the isolation valve upstream and drain the system to prevent freeze damage. Monitor electricity consumption—variable-speed models like the aquastrong smart 45 whole house water pressure booster pump should show reduced energy usage during low-demand periods. If pressure fluctuates rapidly, check tank charge (if equipped) and verify check valve functionality. Always ensure proper voltage supply matches pump specifications (115V vs. 220V). Keep surrounding areas clear for adequate ventilation.
