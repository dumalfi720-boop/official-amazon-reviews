What Is Eletronicos?

A mirrorless camera is a digital imaging device that uses an electronic viewfinder and interchangeable lenses to capture images and video without the mirror mechanism found in traditional DSLRs. Modern mirrorless systems offer advanced autofocus, real-time exposure preview, and compact form factors, making them increasingly popular for professional photography, videography, and content creation across various industries.
