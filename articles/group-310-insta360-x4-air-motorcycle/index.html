Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maximize 360-degree footage longevity by cleaning lens surfaces with microfiber cloths immediately after saltwater or dusty environments—salt crystals and mineral deposits permanently etch glass if allowed to dry. Invest in high-speed UHS-II memory cards rated V90 or above; standard consumer cards create dropped frames during 8K recording. Rotate your camera's mounting orientation weekly during extended travels to prevent uneven wear on gimbal contact points. Store batteries at 40-60% charge in cool environments during off-season storage; full or depleted batteries degrade capacity faster. Create redundant backup workflows—copy footage daily to external drives, never rely solely on cloud services for mission-critical content. Clean sensor ports gently with compressed air after each adventure; accumulated dust triggers thermal throttling and Focus Ring contact degradation.
