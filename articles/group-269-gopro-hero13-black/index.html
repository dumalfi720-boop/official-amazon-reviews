❓ Can I use a GoPro as my primary webcam on Windows?
Yes, all GoPro models support Windows webcam functionality through USB-C connection and official drivers. Requires Windows 10 Build 1909+. Setup involves downloading GoPro software, installing drivers, and selecting GoPro as your video input in OBS or Streamlabs, typically completing in 15-25 minutes.
❓ What do ND filters do on action cameras?
ND (Neutral Density) filters reduce incoming light without changing colors, enabling slower shutter speeds even in bright sunlight. This preserves cinematic motion blur at high frame rates; the included set offers 3-6 stops of light reduction, essential for professional video work in variable lighting conditions.
❓ Is the GoPro HB-Series Macro Lens worth the investment?
The Macro Lens achieves 4cm minimum focus distance, enabling detailed close-up capture impossible with standard lenses. Worth the $100+ investment if you create jewelry, food, nature, or macro content. Casual adventure users and streamers derive minimal benefit from macro capability.
