Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maximize replacement part longevity with these expert maintenance practices: install new filters before original ones appear visibly clogged—airflow degradation accelerates wear on motors. Rinse brush rolls monthly under lukewarm water to remove pet hair and dust accumulation that reduces efficiency. Store unused replacement parts in climate-controlled environments; excessive heat degrades rubber belts and synthetic bristles. For keepow reusable mop pads specifically, wash in cold water separately from other linens and air-dry completely before reinstalling—moisture trapped in the dirt chamber breeds mold. Inspect connection points weekly for debris; misaligned attachments strain motors and shorten appliance lifespan. Label replacement parts with installation dates to track maintenance schedules; most brush rolls need replacement every 8-12 weeks with heavy use, while filters require monthly or bi-monthly refreshing depending on household dust levels.
