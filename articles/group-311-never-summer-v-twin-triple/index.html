❓ How do I choose between rocker and camber snowboards?
Rocker prioritizes float, forgiveness, and ease in powder—ideal for beginners and powder specialists. Camber delivers edge grip, pop, and carving precision—suited for technical riders and hardpack terrain. Hybrid camber (combining both) offers versatility for all-mountain riding across variable conditions. Your choice depends on terrain focus: powder → rocker; hardpack → camber; mixed → hybrid.

❓ What flex rating should I choose for an all-mountain snowboard?
Flex rating (1–10 scale) affects responsiveness and maneuverability. All-mountain boards typically range 5–7: medium-flex (5–6) suits versatility and playfulness; medium-stiff (7–8) prioritizes edge grip and drive. Match flex to ability and style: lighter/progressing riders prefer medium flex; heavier/technical riders prefer medium-stiff. Never Summer offers models across this range for different rider profiles.
❓ How often should I wax my snowboard?
Wax every 3–4 days of regular riding to maintain base speed and protect the sintered base material. Variable conditions (mixed snow types) require more frequent waxing. Off-season storage deserves a final protective wax coat. Quality bases like those on never summer snowboards respond well to consistent maintenance, extending the lifespan and maintaining the performance quality that premium boards deliver.
