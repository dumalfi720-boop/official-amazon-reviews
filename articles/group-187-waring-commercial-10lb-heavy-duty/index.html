Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of your fryer, implement a strict oil filtration schedule to remove food particles. Always preheat the oil to the exact recommended temperature before adding food to prevent oil absorption. Regularly calibrate the thermostat to ensure accuracy and avoid overheating. Additionally, avoid overloading the baskets, as this drops the oil temperature too sharply, resulting in greasy food and longer cooking times.
