Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maintain your livestock equipment with quarterly inspection protocols: check all electrical connections for corrosion, verify hose integrity for cracks or weak points, and calibrate any temperature-dependent systems annually. Clean equipment immediately after use in wet conditions to prevent mineral buildup that reduces efficiency by 15-25%. Store drying systems and breeding equipment in climate-controlled spaces; temperature fluctuations degrade motor seals and calibration instruments. Establish preventive maintenance schedules aligned with your operational seasons—peak drying needs require equipment service during low-activity months. Document all maintenance and repairs in permanent logs; this practice increases resale value and helps predict component replacement timing. Most manufacturer warranties require documented maintenance; procrastination on this task voids protection.
