What Is Sports & Outdoors?

Bone conduction headphones are audio devices that transmit sound through vibrations in the cheekbones directly to the inner ear, bypassing the eardrum. This mechanism allows users to hear their music or calls while maintaining complete situational awareness of their surrounding environment, making them a critical safety tool for outdoor athletes and professionals in high-traffic areas.

Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the performance of bone conduction devices, ensure the transducers are centered precisely on the cheekbones, just in front of the ear. To improve bass response, consider lowering the volume of surrounding ambient noise or using a subtle earplug if complete isolation is desired. Regularly clean the contact points with a dry microfiber cloth to prevent sweat buildup from affecting the vibration transmission and ensure the Bluetooth connection remains optimized by updating the firmware.
