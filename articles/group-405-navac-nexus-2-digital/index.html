What Is Automotivo?

Digital manifold gauges are precision instruments that measure pressure, temperature, and refrigerant levels in HVAC systems, providing real-time data via electronic displays. They connect to service ports on air conditioning and refrigeration units, allowing technicians to diagnose system performance and detect leaks with accuracy impossible using analog gauges alone.

Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Calibrate all gauges quarterly using certified reference standards to maintain ±1% accuracy; store temperature clamps in protective foam to prevent connector wear. Clean touchscreens gently with microfiber cloths—never abrasive materials that scratch protective coating. Keep manifold gauges in climate-controlled storage between 50–95°F to preserve electronic component longevity and prevent moisture condensation inside housings. Before each job, verify Bluetooth pairing (on wireless-enabled units) and confirm all hoses attach securely to prevent refrigerant leaks that corrupt readings. Document serial numbers and purchase dates immediately upon receipt to streamline warranty claims if needed.
