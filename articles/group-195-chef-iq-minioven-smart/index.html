Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of these ovens, always allow the unit to cool before cleaning stainless steel surfaces. For air frying, avoid overcrowding the basket to ensure optimal airflow and consistent crispness. When using steam functions, use distilled water to prevent mineral buildup in the reservoir. Regularly empty crumb trays to prevent smoke and maintain heating element efficiency, and always use oven mitts as exterior panels reach high temperatures during operation.
