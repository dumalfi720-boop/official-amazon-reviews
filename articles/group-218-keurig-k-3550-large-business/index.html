Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of your brewer, always use filtered water to reduce mineral accumulation. Regularly follow the manufacturer's guidelines to descale the system every 3-6 months depending on water hardness. Additionally, wipe down the drip tray daily and ensure the needle is clear of pod debris to prevent clogs and maintain optimal flow rates. These steps ensure consistent taste and prevent costly mechanical failures over time.
