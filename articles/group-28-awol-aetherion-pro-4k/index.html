Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Calibrate color temperature and contrast settings after initial installation, allowing 50 hours of warm-up before finalizing adjustments. Install projectors in spaces with light-blocking capability—blackout shades dramatically enhance perceived contrast regardless of lumen output. For UST projectors, verify mounting bracket compatibility and wall surface quality before purchase; uneven surfaces degrade optical performance. Maintain lens cleanliness monthly using microfiber cloths and optical-grade cleaners. Position air intake away from dust sources. For extended viewing sessions, use eco-mode to reduce thermal stress and extend lamp lifespan.
