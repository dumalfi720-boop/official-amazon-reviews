❓ What's the difference between dual-sided and single-sided power meters?
Dual-sided pedals (like favero assioma DUO models) measure power from both legs separately, revealing power balance asymmetry and pedaling inefficiency. Single-sided meters measure one leg and mathematically double the result, sacrificing balance data but costing 30-40% less. For serious training, dual-sided offers superior insight.
