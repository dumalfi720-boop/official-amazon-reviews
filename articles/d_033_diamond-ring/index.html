Is a 4 carat diamond too big for everyday wear?

It is a prominent stone. If the recipient has an active, hands-on lifestyle (medical, construction, fitness), a lower-profile bezel or flush setting might be safer. However, the cathedral prong setting here is robust enough for typical professional and social daily wear.

How does fluorescence affect this stone?

Stones in this listing typically exhibit None to Faint fluorescence. In the G-H color range, faint fluorescence can actually improve the apparent whiteness in daylight without causing the "milky" haze sometimes seen in higher colors (D-F) with strong fluorescence.
