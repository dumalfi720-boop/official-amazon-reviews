❓ What is the difference between auto-darkening and passive welding helmets?
Auto-darkening helmets use electronic sensors to darken lens opacity in milliseconds, allowing welders to see their joint before striking the arc. Passive helmets stay permanently dark, requiring flip-up motion to see. Auto-darkening reduces eye strain and improves welding accuracy—essential for precision work.
