Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of hybrid ceramic cookware, avoid using metal utensils which can scratch the surface; silicone or wood is recommended. Always allow pans to cool slightly before introducing cold water to prevent thermal shock and warping. For induction users, ensure the base is clean to maintain maximum energy efficiency. Low-to-medium heat is generally sufficient due to the high thermal conductivity of the hybrid construction.
