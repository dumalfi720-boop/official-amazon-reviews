Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Store all kits in cool, dark conditions (bathroom cabinet or refrigerator for hydrogel masks) to preserve active ingredients and prevent oxidation. Follow the prescribed AM/PM sequence—layering in the wrong order reduces efficacy by up to 40%. Apply products to clean, slightly damp skin to enhance absorption.
