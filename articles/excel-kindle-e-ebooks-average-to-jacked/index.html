For beginners, full-body three days weekly or upper/lower four days weekly outperforms body-part splits. These frequencies hit each muscle 2-3 times per week, maximizing protein synthesis windows. Research supports 0.7-1g per pound of bodyweight daily for maximizing muscle growth. A 180lb person needs 130-180g protein. Spread intake across 3-5 meals for optimal muscle protein synthesis. Consistency trumps precision.
When should you increase weight versus reps? ▾
Use double progression: pick a rep range (e.g., 6-10 reps). When you hit the top end for all prescribed sets, increase weight 2.5-5lbs next session. If reps drop below the bottom end, stay at current weight until you climb back up. This auto-regulates progression. Never sacrifice form for weight. If joints hurt, reduce load and focus on tempo. Strength gains in moderate rep ranges drive hypertrophy best.
