Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Inflate your mat in cool morning temperatures to assess actual firmness before layering bedding; afternoon heat expands air, masking true support firmness. Store deflated mats in cool, dry locations away from direct sunlight—UV exposure degrades fabric and accelerates valve brittleness over 12+ months. Use a protective groundsheet beneath your mat to minimize puncture risk from sharp vehicle debris or gravel. Top up firmness every 3–5 days during extended trips; normal use causes slight pressure loss. Periodically inspect valve seals by applying soapy water; bubbling indicates developing leaks requiring repair-kit intervention.

Self-inflating mats expand automatically when the valve opens, requiring no pump for initial setup. Non-self-inflating mats require hand or electric pumps for all inflation.
❓ What R-value do I need for winter vehicle camping?
For temperatures below 32°F, select mats with R-values of 4.0 or higher. The exped megamat achieves R-value ~5.0; most Exped products clearly publish ratings. Combine with insulated bedding (down comforters, synthetic quilts) for cold-weather comfort. R-values below 3.0 suit only three-season use in mild climates; higher R-values ensure thermal protection in alpine and winter environments.
❓ How do I prevent air leaks in my car sleeping mat?
