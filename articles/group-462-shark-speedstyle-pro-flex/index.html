Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maintain professional hair dryers by cleaning ionic emitters monthly with distilled water to prevent mineral buildup that reduces ion output. Store with cord loosely coiled (avoid sharp bends) to preserve longevity. Use lower heat settings on fine or color-treated hair; higher heat on thick or curly textures. Allow 2-3 minute cool-down periods between clients in salon settings to prevent thermal stress on motors. Apply heat protectant products before drying to minimize cuticle damage. Never cover air intake vents during operation, as blockages cause motor strain. Store in dry environments; avoid humid bathrooms that promote mineral deposit accumulation.
