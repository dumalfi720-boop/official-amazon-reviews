Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of vinyl decals and fabric wallpapers, ensure the wall is cleaned with a damp cloth and fully dried before application to prevent bubbling. For bedding sets, wash the quilt and sheets in cold water on a gentle cycle to preserve the fabric's integrity. When installing large murals, use a level and a plumb line to ensure the cartoon characters are perfectly aligned across the wall seams.
