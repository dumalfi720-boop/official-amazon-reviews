Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of your machine, perform a descale cycle every 3 months to prevent calcium buildup. Use filtered water to protect the heating elements and ensure flavor purity. For the best espresso, calibrate your grinder settings based on the roast date of your beans; lighter roasts generally require a finer grind to avoid under-extraction.
