Key Features That Define Quality

Certified Center Stones (The Trust Factor)

The most critical feature is documentation. These earrings feature natural diamonds accompanied by a GIA (Gemological Institute of America) grading report. This certificate details the 4Cs—Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat weight—verifying exactly what you are paying for. The stones typically range in the H-I Color / SI1-SI2 Clarity sweet spot: white to the naked eye with inclusions invisible without 10x magnification.

Precision Cut for Maximum Fire

Cut is the only "C" controlled by human hands, and it dictates sparkle. These studs utilize an Excellent/Ideal Cut grade, optimizing the angles and proportions to return maximum light to the viewer. The result is a distinct "hearts and arrows" pattern symmetry visible under a scope, translating to intense brilliance (white light) and fire (rainbow dispersion) in real-world lighting.

Secure & Comfortable Settings

Crafted in your choice of 14k White Gold, Yellow Gold, or Platinum, the settings utilize a four-prong basket design. This minimizes metal visibility, making the diamonds appear larger, while ensuring the girdle is protected. The included **screw-back findings** are a non-negotiable safety feature for valuable studs, preventing the "lost earring" heartbreak common with friction backs.
