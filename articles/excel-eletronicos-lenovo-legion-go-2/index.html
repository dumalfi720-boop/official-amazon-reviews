Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the key upgrades in the legion go 2 compared to the original model? ▾
The legion go 2 upgrades to an AMD Ryzen Z2 Extreme APU with RDNA 3.5 graphics, delivering roughly 30% higher GPU performance than the original's Z1 Extreme. It adds a larger 74Wh battery (up from 49.2Wh), 144Hz VRR display support, Hall-effect joysticks to eliminate drift, and a refined detachable controller mechanism with stronger magnets. Windows 11 comes pre-installed with an optimized Legion Space 2.0 UI. These improvements make it a compelling upgrade for existing owners seeking better battery life and controller reliability.
How does the legion go 2 perform in AAA gaming titles at native resolution? ▾
At native 2560x1600, the legion go 2 averages 35-45 FPS in demanding titles like Alan Wake 2 or Cyberpunk 2077 on Low/Medium settings at 25W TDP. Enabling FSR 3.1 Quality mode boosts this to 55-65 FPS with minimal visual artifacts. For competitive shooters like Valorant or Apex Legends, you'll hit 120+ FPS at 1200p Low, fully utilizing the 144Hz panel. Dialing TDP to 30W plugged in yields near-60 FPS native in many 2023-2024 AAA games.
Is the legion go 2 worth buying for someone who already owns a Steam Deck OLED? ▾
