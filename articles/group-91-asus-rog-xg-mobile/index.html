What Is Informatica?

External graphics cards refer to portable GPU enclosures that connect to laptops and desktops via Thunderbolt or USB interfaces, delivering dedicated graphics processing power without requiring internal hardware modifications. These devices enable users to upgrade graphics capabilities on the fly, particularly benefiting portable workstations and gaming laptops seeking performance expansion without physical constraints.
