Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Implement RAID-6 or equivalent redundancy across all platforms to protect against multi-drive failures. Monitor thermal conditions carefully; enterprise deployments often operate in non-climate-controlled environments requiring proactive cooling management. Establish regular snapshot schedules independent of backup regimens—snapshots protect against ransomware and accidental data modifications. Configure 10GbE networks where available; gigabit Ethernet becomes restrictive above 50MB/s sustained throughput. Implement firmware update schedules during low-utilization windows. Document storage architecture and capacity planning assumptions to facilitate future scalability.
