The Versatility Factor: Customizing Your Finish

One of the most frequent questions regarding this product is how to make dewy foundation matte without losing the skincare benefits. The answer lies in application technique. Because the base is so emollient, it takes powder exceptionally well. For a soft-matte, velvety finish, apply with a damp beauty sponge, let it set for 60 seconds, and press a finely milled setting powder over the T-zone and cheekbones. Alternatively, mixing a single drop of a mattifying primer into the foundation on the back of your hand before application transforms the finish entirely. Mastering how to make a dewy foundation matte effectively gives you two foundations in one bottle—radiant for day, polished for evening.
