Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of your machine, perform a descaling cycle every 30-60 days using a vinegar-water solution or a commercial descaler to remove mineral buildup. Always use filtered water to improve taste and protect the heating element. For the best flavor, grind your beans immediately before brewing and use a digital scale to maintain a consistent ratio, ensuring every pot meets professional standards regardless of the machine used.
