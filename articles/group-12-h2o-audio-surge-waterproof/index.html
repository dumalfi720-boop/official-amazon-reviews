Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Proper ear fitting dramatically influences comfort and waterproof effectiveness—spend time identifying the correct ear tip size before vigorous activity. Rinse headphones with fresh water after saltwater exposure to prevent mineral accumulation that degrades acoustic performance. Store in the provided carrying case between uses to protect delicate components and maintain cable integrity. For swimmers, insert headphones immediately before water entry to minimize air pocket formation that reduces seal effectiveness. Test waterproof integrity with brief submersion before extended training sessions. During winter training, allow condensation to evaporate naturally rather than applying heat that could damage internal components. Rotate between multiple ear tip sizes to accommodate natural ear canal variation throughout the day.
