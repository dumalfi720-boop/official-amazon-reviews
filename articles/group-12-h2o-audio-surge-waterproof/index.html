Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maximize waterproof headphone longevity by rinsing with fresh water immediately post-saltwater exposure, ensuring complete driver drainage before storage, and rotating between multiple pairs to allow thorough moisture evaporation cycles. Store units in designated dry cases with desiccant packets during extended non-use periods. Periodically test Bluetooth connectivity in your primary swimming location, as mineral deposits occasionally affect reception despite manufacturer waterproofing claims. Apply protective earpieces seasonally and replace fit tips annually regardless of visible wear, as degraded silicone compromises water sealing integrity. Avoid exposing units to temperature extremes and prolonged direct sunlight.
