Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Optimize gaming performance by enabling XMP/DOCP profiles in BIOS to activate DDR5 RAM rated speeds, immediately improving frame rates in CPU-bound scenarios. Monitor thermals during initial gaming sessions using manufacturer-provided software; sustained GPU temperatures above 80°C indicate potential airflow optimization opportunities within your case. Maintain SSD health through periodic TRIM operations and avoid filling NVMe drives beyond 85% capacity, which degrades write performance.
