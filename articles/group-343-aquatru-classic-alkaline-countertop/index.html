🔍 People Also Ask

❓ Do countertop water purifiers actually remove PFOA and PFOS forever chemicals?
Yes. Reverse osmosis systems like AquaTru are NSF-certified to remove >99% of PFOA/PFOS molecules. These forever chemicals cannot pass through RO membranes, making countertop purifiers one of few affordable home solutions for PFAS contamination. Carbon filters alone do not remove forever chemicals; reverse osmosis is required for verified removal.
❓ How long do water purifier filters last before replacement?
Countertop RO filters typically last 3-6 months (3,000-5,000 gallons) depending on incoming water quality and household consumption. Hard water or high contamination shortens lifespan; municipal treated water extends it. Most manufacturers recommend quarterly replacement for consistent performance. AquaTru one-year combo packs bundle four quarterly replacements for budgeting predictability.
❓ Can you install a countertop water purifier without a plumber?
Absolutely. Countertop systems like AquaTru require zero plumbing expertise. Installation involves attaching quick-disconnect hoses to your faucet aerator and placing drain lines in a sink—15 minutes maximum. No drilling, permanent modifications, or professional help needed. This DIY-friendly approach makes countertop purifiers ideal for renters and homeowners avoiding installation costs.
❓ Is reverse osmosis water safe to drink every day?
Yes, with one caveat: pure RO water lacks minerals your body needs. This is why quality RO systems include remineralization stages adding beneficial calcium and magnesium back into finished water. AquaTru's 4-stage system delivers remineralized water at WHO-recommended mineral levels (80-120 ppm), making it safe and nutritionally appropriate for daily consumption.
