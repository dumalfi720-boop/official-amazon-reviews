❓ What is the difference between pure sine wave and modified sine wave inverters?
Pure sine wave inverters generate smooth AC output matching grid electricity, protecting sensitive electronics and extending appliance lifespan by 30–50%. Modified sine wave alternatives create square-wave approximations that cause humming in audio equipment, reduce motor efficiency by 10–15%, and can damage computer power supplies. victron energy exclusively manufactures pure sine wave inverters because their customer base demands reliability over cost minimization.

❓ Can inverter chargers run 240V appliances in a 120V system?

For pure 240V requirements, specify 120V/240V split-phase equipment during system design; retrofitting is complex and expensive.
