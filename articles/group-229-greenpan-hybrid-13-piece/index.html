Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To extend the life of your cookware, always preheat on low to medium heat before adding ingredients. Avoid using aerosol cooking sprays, as they can create a gummy residue that ruins the nonstick surface. For hybrid sets, while metal utensils are permissible, using silicone or wood still preserves the finish longer. Always allow pans to cool slightly before washing to prevent thermal shock, which can compromise the structural integrity of the bond.
