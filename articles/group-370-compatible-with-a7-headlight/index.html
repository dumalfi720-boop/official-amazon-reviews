🔍 People Also Ask

❓ What's the difference between LED and halogen headlight assemblies?
LED assemblies deliver 40-60% greater brightness (1,600-2,200 lumens vs. 700-1,000 lumens), produce daylight-white 6,500K color versus yellow 3,000K halogen, operate at lower temperatures (60-85°C vs. 200-250°C), and last 15,000+ hours versus 1,000 hours. LED provides superior night visibility and reduced heat-related lens cracking.
❓ How long does headlight assembly installation typically take?
Installation time ranges 35-90 minutes depending on vehicle architecture. Tesla Model 3/Y requires 35 minutes (wheel well liner removal). Audi A7 takes 45 minutes per side. Volvo VNL and Mercedes GLA require 90 minutes per side due to bumper removal or complex mounting. Professional shop installation adds labor costs of $200-400 per side.
❓ Do aftermarket LED headlights work with factory electrical systems?
Most full-LED assemblies integrate directly with OEM wiring harnesses via weatherproof connectors. Tesla assemblies feature CAN-bus compatibility to prevent diagnostic errors. Some vehicles require ballast module installation (Audi). Verify electrical compatibility before purchase by confirming part numbers and consulting installation guides or manufacturer support.
❓ What warranty coverage should I expect on LED headlight assemblies?
Standard warranties cover 2-3 years for manufacturing defects, sealed housing integrity, and electrical function. Coverage excludes collision damage, improper installation, and bulb cycles. Tesla and Mercedes offer extended diagnostics coverage through manufacturer networks. Aftermarket brands provide direct manufacturer support; verify return policies and warranty claim procedures before purchase.
❓ Are LED headlights legal and DOT compliant?
Factory LED assemblies (like Tesla and Mercedes original equipment) are fully DOT and SAE compliant. Aftermarket LED conversions must meet specific beam pattern and lux output requirements documented in FMVSS 108 standards. The assemblies reviewed (Audi, Volvo, Tesla, Mercedes) achieve full compliance; verify local regulations in your jurisdiction before installation.
