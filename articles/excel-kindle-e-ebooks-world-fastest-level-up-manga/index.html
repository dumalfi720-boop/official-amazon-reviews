When should I start attempting full runs versus practicing individual segments? ▾

Rushing into full runs too early builds bad habits and wastes time on resets. Structure your sessions: spend 70% of practice time on your weakest segment using save states or practice tools, then finish with 2-3 full attempts to test stamina and routing memory. Track segment success rates in a spreadsheet—when all segments hit 80% consistency, increase full-run frequency.
