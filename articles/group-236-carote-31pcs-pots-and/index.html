Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of your granite cookware, always use silicone or wooden utensils to avoid scratching the titanium coating. Avoid using aerosol cooking sprays, as these can create a sticky residue that degrades nonstick performance over time. Always allow the pans to cool slightly before washing with warm soapy water and a soft sponge, and avoid using high-heat settings for prolonged periods without food in the pan to prevent overheating.
