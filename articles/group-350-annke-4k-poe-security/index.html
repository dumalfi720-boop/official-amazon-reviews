❓ How many cameras do I need for home security?
Coverage depends on property size and risk areas. Commercial properties require more: a 5,000 sq ft retail space typically needs 6–8 cameras. ❓ Is PoE security camera installation difficult?
PoE installation is significantly simpler than traditional analog systems. Mount cameras, run Cat6 cabling from each camera to a central PoE switch (the switch provides both data and power), connect the NVR to the same switch, and configure network settings—typically 90 minutes for a six-camera installation by someone with basic networking knowledge. The main requirement is proximity to your network switch; outdoor camera placement may demand conduit runs through walls or along soffits. Most difficulties arise from network configuration rather than physical installation.
❓ What storage capacity do I need for security cameras?
Storage requirements scale with resolution, camera count, and retention needs. A six-camera 4K system using H.265+ compression requires approximately 250–300 GB per day. The standard 2TB drive provides 8 days of continuous recording before overwriting; many users add 4TB or 8TB drives for 15–30 day retention. Calculate based on your priority: identify critical events (break-ins, accidents) typically occur during specific hours, so 7–14 day retention often suffices. Professional installations frequently upgrade to 8TB+ drives or NAS systems with RAID redundancy for critical applications.
❓ Can I access my security cameras remotely from my phone?
All modern systems including annke provide mobile app remote viewing, accessible from anywhere with internet connectivity. Cameras stream to the NVR, which serves footage to your phone via app authentication. Quality depends on your home internet upload speed: 5–10 Mbps supports smooth remote viewing of 1–2 simultaneous camera streams. Verify the app supports your phone type (iOS or Android), confirm the system supports remote access without mandatory cloud subscriptions (critical for privacy), and test connectivity before finalizing installation to identify network configuration issues.
