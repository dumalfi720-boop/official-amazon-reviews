❓ What is the difference between ionic and infrared hair dryer technology?
Ionic technology emits negatively charged ions neutralizing positive charges creating frizz, resulting in smoother hair with reduced static. Infrared technology uses infrared light wavelengths penetrating the hair shaft from inside, evaporating moisture gently without surface heat damage. Combined in the srilabs dryq smart hair dryer, they reduce drying time while protecting hair integrity—delivering professional results with less heat exposure.
