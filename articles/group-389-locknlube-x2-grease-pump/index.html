What Is Locknlube?

Air-powered grease pump kits are industrial dispensing systems designed to efficiently transfer and distribute lubricating grease from large pails or drums into machinery, equipment, and components. These pneumatic-driven pumps use compressed air to generate high-pressure output, enabling rapid, consistent grease delivery at rates measured in ounces per minute, significantly reducing manual labor and improving application precision in manufacturing, automotive, and maintenance environments.

Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

Maintain peak performance through monthly air line inspection—check for moisture accumulation in regulators, which degrades pump seal longevity and causes pressure inconsistency. Store grease pump systems in climate-controlled environments (50-85°F optimal) to prevent seal brittleness and housing corrosion. Before each multi-hour operation session, verify hose connections are torqued to manufacturer specification; loose couplers introduce 12-15% efficiency losses and create safety hazards under sustained pressure. Change air filters every 100 operating hours (or quarterly, whichever comes first) to prevent particulate contamination that damages internal pump mechanisms. When transitioning between grease grades, flush the system with compatible solvent to prevent viscosity incompatibility issues that cause backpressure spikes.

🔍 People Also Ask

❓ What PSI do I need for an air grease pump?
Most industrial operations thrive at 8,000-10,000 PSI outlet pressure, which balances speed and precision. Check your air compressor's CFM rating (typically 10-25 CFM required); undersized compressors may struggle maintaining pressure during continuous operation. Premium systems deliver 10,000 PSI for 30-40% faster dispensing.
❓ How long does a 5-gallon grease pail last with an air pump?
At 16 oz/min flow rate, a full 35 lb pail dispenses in approximately 35-40 minutes of continuous pumping. Actual duration varies based on nozzle type, grease viscosity, and application technique. High-volume fleet operations typically consume one pail per 7-10 days depending on vehicle count and maintenance intervals.
❓ Can I use any grease with an air-powered pump?
Most lithium and calcium-based greases (ISO viscosity 100-220) work reliably. Ultra-thick specialty greases or synthetic formulations may require higher PSI (9,000+) to dispense smoothly. Always verify grease compatibility with pump seal materials; incompatible formulations degrade seals within 50-100 operating hours.
❓ How often should I maintain my grease pump?
Change air filters every 100 operating hours or quarterly. Inspect hose connections monthly for looseness or cracks. Replace pump seals annually or every 5,000 operating hours. Store in climate-controlled environments (50-85°F) to prevent seal brittleness and housing corrosion between seasonal use.
❓ What's the difference between 8,000 and 10,000 PSI grease pumps?
10,000 PSI systems dispense 25-35% faster (16 oz/min vs 12 oz/min typical) and overcome longer hose runs more effectively. However, they demand higher-capacity air compressors and more robust hoses. Choose 8,000 PSI for budget-conscious shops with shorter hose runs; 10,000 PSI for high-volume operations prioritizing speed.
