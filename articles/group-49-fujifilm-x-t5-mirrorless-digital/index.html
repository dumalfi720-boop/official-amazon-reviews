What Is Eletronicos?

Mirrorless digital cameras refer to interchangeable-lens cameras that eliminate the traditional mirror mechanism found in DSLRs, using electronic viewfinders and sensor-based autofocus instead. This technology enables more compact bodies, faster continuous shooting, and real-time exposure preview, making mirrorless systems increasingly dominant in professional and enthusiast photography markets.
