What Is Eletrodomesticos?

Automatic coffee machines are integrated appliances designed to automate the process of grinding beans, tamping, and brewing espresso. These machines refer to systems that utilize an internal brewing unit and precise pressure pumps to ensure consistent extraction, often featuring one-touch capabilities for milk-based beverages and automated cleaning cycles for long-term maintenance.

Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

To maximize the lifespan of your machine, always use filtered water to prevent calcium buildup. Regularly descale the system according to the manufacturer's prompts and use high-quality Arabica beans to avoid clogging the grinder. For milk-based drinks, purge the steam wand immediately after use to prevent milk residue from hardening, which can obstruct the nozzle and affect the texture of your foam.
