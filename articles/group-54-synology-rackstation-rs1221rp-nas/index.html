What Is Informatica?

Enterprise network-attached storage (NAS) refers to dedicated server systems designed to provide centralized data storage, backup, and file-sharing capabilities across organizational networks. These systems combine specialized operating systems, redundant hardware components, and multi-user management features to ensure data accessibility, security, and business continuity for medium to large-scale operations.
