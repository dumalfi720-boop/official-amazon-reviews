No, govee permanent outdoor lights cannot be cut — each 32.8-foot strand is a continuous FPC (flexible printed circuit) with IC-controlled LEDs addressed individually. For custom lengths, plan runs using the 2.6-inch LED spacing: measure soffit, divide by 2.6 inches, round down to nearest LED, and use the included end-cap terminator on the last node. Excess length can be coiled behind fascia or tucked into soffit returns; the minimum bend radius is 1.2 inches without damaging the FPC.
