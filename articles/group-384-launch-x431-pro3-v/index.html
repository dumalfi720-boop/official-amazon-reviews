What Is Automotivo?

Diagnostic automotive scanning tools are specialized devices that connect to vehicle onboard diagnostic (OBD) systems to read fault codes, perform system tests, and enable bidirectional control of vehicle components. These professional-grade instruments range from basic code readers to advanced multi-protocol scanners supporting heavy-duty trucks, passenger vehicles, and emerging electric vehicle architectures.
