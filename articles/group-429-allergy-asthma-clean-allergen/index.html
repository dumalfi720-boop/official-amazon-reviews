Tips to Maximize Your Product's Lifespan

For optimal results, layer your approach: use the allergen spray on pet bedding, furniture, and high-traffic areas weekly, then run a HEPA purifier continuously in sleeping spaces and living areas. Position purifiers away from walls and obstructions to maximize airflow circulation. Replace filters on manufacturer schedules (typically 6–12 months) to maintain effectiveness; clogged filters reduce airflow and increase noise. In high-allergen seasons or pet-heavy households, increase spray application to twice weekly. Keep purifier intake vents dust-free by vacuuming around units monthly. For wall-mounted models, ensure mounting height allows air circulation above and below the unit.
