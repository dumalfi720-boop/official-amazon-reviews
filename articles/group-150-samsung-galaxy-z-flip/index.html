What Is Cell Phones & Accessories?

Foldable smartphones are mobile devices featuring flexible OLED displays that allow the handset to fold in half, combining the portability of a compact device with the screen real estate of a traditional smartphone. A defining characteristic is the mechanical hinge system that enables a 'Flex Mode' for hands-free operation and enhanced multitasking capabilities.
